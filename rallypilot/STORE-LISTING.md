# RallyPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $19.99/month membership — flat, every event included. No percentage of a five-figure corporate day, ever. (Operators running RangePilot too pay $49.98/mo combined for a complete back office — still less than one booking's platform percentage.)

**Tagline:** The event book for outfitters' group and corporate business: build the package, quote it with tiers, chase the waivers, run the day off one sheet, and close the balance.

**What it is:** Groups are the highest-value bookings at every adventure destination — and the only segment with no tooling at all. Individuals book slots online at about 6% a booking; a 40-person corporate day is still assembled from email threads, phone calls, and a printed sheet that cannot know a vehicle went down this morning. RallyPilot is that workflow finished: a package builder so a quote is assembled from your own activities, per-head or per-group pricing, add-ons (extra laps, passenger passes, media packages, catering, transport), minimums, and staffing requirements instead of re-typed per inquiry; a quote book with tiers, deposit terms, a status pipeline, and a follow-up clock on every unanswered quote (orange at two days, red at three) — one press turns a won quote into the event file with the total and deposit carried over; the event file itself — organizer, quote reference, headcount expected vs confirmed, deposit and balance ledger with due-date clocks, special requirements (dietary, transport, AV, awards); a roster and waiver chase list that names exactly who has not signed, exportable as the reminder list for the organizer; a run-of-day planner — groups rotating across activities and stations, staff per block, meal and buffer blocks — with a printable staff day sheet; a day-of add-on ledger so the final invoice matches what actually happened on the ground; a balance close and event report (quoted vs final, per-head realized revenue); and an organizer book — who books annually, typical size, lifetime value, rebook prompts — owned by you, not rented from a platform. Seeded with a fictional corporate event mid-flight: a quote sent two days ago already on its follow-up clock, a 40-head event twelve days out with 31 of 40 waivers in, and its balance clock orange.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no consumer online booking and no payments (the platforms can keep their percentage turf); no CRM or marketing-automation suite; no venue-rental management (that is VenuePilot's book — RallyPilot runs the activity program inside the day); no attendee app or registration portal; no contract drafting — it records the references and terms you set.

**Private by design:** local JSON on the operator's own computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — client lists and pricing never leave the machine, in 100 languages.

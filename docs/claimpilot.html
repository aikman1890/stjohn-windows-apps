ClaimPilot

$14.99/month · Cancel anytime · Bankruptcy Plus $9.99/month · Legal Matters $25.99/month

ClaimPilot is an on-device self-help legal toolkit for Windows. Spot possible debt-collector violations in plain English, build professional validation and dispute letters, organize a small-claims worksheet, study plain-English Section 1983 civil-rights guides, find any of the 94 U.S. federal district courts, and keep a private evidence vault. Everything runs and stays on your PC.

  • Debt-collector violation spotter mapped to FDCPA/FCRA sections
  • Complete letter templates — save, export to PDF, or copy
  • Section 1983 civil-rights guides with honest notes on defenses and deadlines
  • 94-district court finder and deadline tracking with local reminders
  • Bankruptcy Plus: Chapter 7 & 13 guides, checklists, required-courses tracker
  • Legal Matters add-on: traffic tickets, criminal cases, federal matters, county/municipal/district courts, any-case builder

ClaimPilot is not a lawyer and does not give legal advice. It provides general legal information and document tools.

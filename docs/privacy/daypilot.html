DayPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. DayPilot collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising identifiers. The app works fully offline.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in DayPilot — notes, reminders, briefing preferences, settings — is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app, your local data is removed with it.

On-device AI

DayPilot can use on-device AI features provided by Windows. Any text you choose to summarize is processed locally on your device and is never sent to a server.

Voice features

DayPilot offers two optional voice features, both using your device's microphone:

  • Dictation — on the Notes page, the Dictate button turns your speech into text inserted into the note.
  • Talk to DayPilot — on the Briefing page, you can speak commands ("remind me to…", "take a note…", "what's my day look like") and hear a spoken reply.

Spoken audio is processed by the Windows speech services on your device. DayPilot itself never stores, transmits, or shares your voice audio — recognition happens locally and the audio is discarded. The text you dictate is saved only where you choose (your notes, reminders, or task list, all on this device).

Voice lookups ("look up…", "search for…") open the query in your default web browser; that search is handled by the search provider's own privacy policy.

Microphone access is requested only when you tap a voice button, and you can revoke it at any time in Windows Settings. DayPilot has no camera features and never accesses your camera.

Subscriptions and payments

If you purchase a DayPilot subscription, payment is processed by the store or payment provider you buy through. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

DayPilot is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.

ClaimPilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. ClaimPilot collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising identifiers. The app works fully offline.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in ClaimPilot — claims, documents, certificates — is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app, your local data is removed with it.

On-device AI

ClaimPilot can use on-device AI features provided by Windows. Any text you choose to process is handled locally on your device and is never sent to a server.

Payments

If you purchase ClaimPilot or an add-on, payment is processed by the store or payment provider you buy through. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

ClaimPilot is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.

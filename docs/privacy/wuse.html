wuse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. wuse collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising identifiers. The app works fully offline.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in wuse is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the app, your local data is removed with it.

Microphone

If you use voice input, wuse listens through your microphone only while the microphone button shows it is listening. Your speech is converted to text by the Windows speech service on your device. wuse stores no audio recordings and transmits no audio anywhere. Voice transcripts you send become chat messages, stored only on your device like the rest of your data.

Password vault

If you save logins in the Vault, they are stored in the Windows Credential Locker — encrypted by Windows and tied to your Windows user account. wuse never writes passwords to files, never shows them on screen, never speaks them aloud, and never includes them in AI requests. Copying a password puts it on your clipboard, which wuse clears automatically after 30 seconds.

Email and calendar (optional)

Email and calendar features are off until you connect an account. If you connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook/Hotmail/Office 365, or Yahoo Mail, wuse uses standard OAuth sign-in (Google/Microsoft) or an app password you provide (Yahoo) to read your mail and calendar and — only with your explicit "yes" each time — send mail or create events. Sign-in tokens are kept encrypted on your device (Windows Credential Locker / DPAPI); your Yahoo app password is stored only in the encrypted vault. wuse never logs email bodies and never sends your mail or calendar data to us or anyone else. You can disconnect an account at any time; wuse then discards its saved credentials.

Payments

If you purchase wuse, payment is processed by the store or payment provider you buy through. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

wuse is not directed at children under 13, and we knowingly collect no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.

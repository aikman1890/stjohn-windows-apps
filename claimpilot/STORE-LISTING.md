## Description

ClaimPilot is an on-device self-help legal toolkit for Windows. Spot possible
debt-collector violations in plain English, build professional validation,
cease-communication, and credit-bureau dispute letters, organize a small-claims
worksheet, study plain-English Section 1983 civil-rights guides, find any of
the 94 U.S. federal district courts, track deadlines with local reminders, and
keep a private evidence vault with an exportable packet. The optional
Bankruptcy Plus unlock adds plain-English Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 guides,
document checklists, bankruptcy deadline presets, and a tracker for the two
courses bankruptcy law requires (pre-filing credit counseling and pre-discharge
debtor education) with a certificate vault. Everything runs and stays on your
PC — no account, no cloud, no uploads.

ClaimPilot is not a lawyer and does not give legal advice. It provides general
legal information and document tools. Verify everything with your court's rules
or an attorney.

## Feature bullets

- Debt-collector toolkit: violation spotter mapped to FDCPA/FCRA sections, plus complete letter templates you can save as TXT, export to PDF, or copy.
- Civil-rights self-help: six plain-English Section 1983 claim guides with honest notes on defenses and deadlines, a 94-district court finder, and pro se resources.
- Private by design: deadlines, evidence, and timelines stored only on your PC, with local toast reminders and a one-click evidence-packet PDF export.
- Bankruptcy Plus (optional $9.99 in-app purchase): Chapter 7 & 13 plain-English guides, document checklists, bankruptcy deadline presets, and a required-courses tracker with certificate vault.

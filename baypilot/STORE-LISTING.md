# BayPilot - Store Listing Draft

**Tagline:** The repair-order board that remembers every car - and every "no."

**Price:** $19.99/month

**Description:**
BayPilot is the working system for a small auto repair shop (1-3 bays) that runs on paper tickets and memory today. A dashboard shows the day at a glance - cars in the shop, what was opened, picked up, invoiced, and collected. The board tracks every repair order through the real pipeline: checked in, diagnosing, waiting approval, approved / in progress, waiting parts, done, picked up - with promised dates and who is waiting on whom.

Every vehicle gets a permanent file: its full service history - approved AND declined work, with dollar amounts, declined reasons, and recommendations for next time. When a car comes back, the open recommendations from its last visit are right there on the new ticket.

Estimates are built line by line (labor at your rate, parts at your cost plus your markup - your numbers only; there is no labor guide and no invented data), and every customer decision lands in an approval log with the date, method, who approved, and any decline reason - the shop's dispute record. Declined work goes into a register that keeps the call-back list alive with follow-up dates, instead of dying on a sticky note. Reminders surface cars past their promised time, follow-ups that are due, and vehicles reaching the service intervals you set.

Invoices are created from approved lines, payments are recorded (never processed), and everything - estimates, invoices, vehicle files - prints or exports from inside the app. All data stays on the shop's PC in local JSON files. No account, no cloud, no network calls.

**Features:**
- End-of-day dashboard: opened, picked up, invoiced, collected, approvals pending
- Repair-order board with the real shop pipeline and promised dates
- Permanent vehicle files: full history including declined work and recommendations
- Line-item estimates with your labor rate, your markup, supplies, and tax
- Approval log: decision, date, method, who approved, decline reasons
- Declined-work register with follow-up dates and overdue flags
- Printable estimate and invoice export (HTML)
- Reminders for late promises, due follow-ups, and shop-set service intervals

**Privacy:** All data is stored locally on your PC. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

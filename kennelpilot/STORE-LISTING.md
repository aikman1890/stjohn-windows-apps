# KennelPilot - Store Listing Draft

**Tagline:** The kennel board that checks the vaccine paperwork before the dog is at the door.

**Price:** $19.99/month

**Description:**
KennelPilot is the working system for a small boarding kennel or pet-sitting operation. The board shows who is in the house, who arrives today, and who goes home today, against your run capacity. Reservations flow from booked to checked in to checked out - and every stay opens with the vaccine gate.

The gate is the point. Each pet file carries its vaccination records with the expiry dates from the owner's paperwork. When you book a stay, KennelPilot checks those dates against the stay: expired or missing records stop the booking until you fix the file or record an explicit override with your reason - so the renewal conversation happens at booking time, never as a doorstep surprise. A vaccine watch page keeps 30/60/90-day lookahead lists so you can call owners while there is still time.

Care is logged as it happens: feeding, water, yard time, medications, health notes, incidents - timestamped and signed with staff initials, exportable as a per-stay day sheet. Pet files hold feeding instructions, medications, behavior notes, the owner's emergency contact, and the vet. Billing builds the stay invoice from your nightly rates (including a second-pet rate), baths, and medication days, and records payments without ever touching card data.

Everything lives on the kennel's PC in local JSON files. No account, no cloud, no network calls - and a sample owner, pet, and stay are seeded on first run so you can see the board work before entering a single real record.

**Features:**
- Kennel board: in-house count vs capacity, arrivals and departures today
- Vaccine gate at booking: expired/missing records block, with a reasoned-override trail
- Vaccine watch: expired + 30/60/90-day expiry lists and missing-record pets
- Pet files: feeding, medications, behavior, vet, emergency contact, vaccine documents
- Timestamped care log with staff-initial sign-off and printable day sheets
- Stay billing from your rates, with payment recording and invoice export

**Privacy:** All data is stored locally on your PC. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

# YachtPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $39.99/month membership — flat. No per-vessel tiers, because we don't rent by the hull.

**Tagline:** Your whole management book — vessels, certificates, maintenance, charters, owner money — on your own machine, working with no signal.

**What it is:** Yacht platforms rent by the hull: from $200/mo per vessel for the ISM incumbent, €120–€220/mo per vessel plus a €50 ISM add-on for the newer one — a five-boat management company pays €600–€1,350 a month before typing a record, for software whose daily job is lists and clocks. So small managers run on four spreadsheets and a WhatsApp group, and owners get reports assembled by hand. YachtPilot is that stack finished: a vessel file per yacht (flag, berth, owner, insurance/registration clocks, document vault of local paths); a crew certificate engine — STCW, ENG1 medicals, licences, passports, visas — warning at 30 days because renewals mean courses and doctors booked weeks out; a charter-ready gate that REFUSES to call a vessel ready while an assigned certificate is expired or maintenance is overdue, overridable only with a dated note on the record; maintenance clocks by date or engine hours (engine service, haul-out, zincs, liferaft and flare dates, rig inspections); a charter/owner-use/yard calendar with hard overlap blocks, certificate-window flags, and APA advance-vs-spent tracking; an owner expense ledger in explicit multi-currency decimals (EUR/USD/GBP — never assumed) that generates the monthly owner statement instead of you assembling it; a vendor and yard book with COI expiry clocks; and a per-vessel proof ledger — certificates renewed, services completed, charters run, statements sent — exportable when an owner asks what you've actually done this quarter. Seeded with two fictional sample vessels: one charter-ready with a charter booked, one gated by an expired ENG1 and an overdue liferaft service, so the gate and the statement both work before you enter a thing.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no AIS tracking, no weather, no navigation; no ISM/MLC/flag-state compliance certification — it tracks the dates and documents you enter and flags expiries, and certification remains the operator's responsibility; no charter marketplace, no payments, no crew payroll, no owner or crew portals, and not a marina berth-billing system.

**Private by design:** local JSON on the manager's PC. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — fully offline, which in this business is not a limitation but the headline: the book works at sea, in the yard, and in the marina dead zone, in 100 languages.

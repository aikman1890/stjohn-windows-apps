# EntityPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $9.99/month membership — flat, unlimited entities.

**Tagline:** One missed annual report can dissolve a company. This is the calendar that doesn't miss.

**What it is:** Accounting software charges per company — QuickBooks Online runs roughly $38–$275/mo per entity, and true multi-entity tools start around $375/mo — while charging nothing at all for the thing that actually kills small companies: the deadline register. Owners of 2–10 LLCs and corps keep existence itself in a spreadsheet: every entity's annual report, franchise tax, registered-agent renewal, and DBA date, each state on its own month, portal, and penalty — miss one and the state administratively dissolves the company, and you find out when a bank or a title company bounces. EntityPilot is that register finished: an entity book (type, state, file number, fiscal year end, status as YOU last confirmed it with the date — never a fake live lookup); a deadline engine across all entities with the line's green/orange/red color language and one consolidated "next 90 days, everywhere" calendar; a registered-agent book with fee/renewal dates, correspondence log, and portal pointers (passwords are never stored — the field is a note like "password in vault"); a versioned ownership register whose active stakes validate their own sum to 100% per class, plus officers and managers; an intercompany loan/transfer/repayment ledger with the live balance per pair; a document index pointing at your own formation docs, agreements, and EIN letters; and a portfolio dashboard that puts anything overdue at the top. State starter templates (TX, WY, DE, FL, CA, NV) ship labeled as starting points with review dates — confirm every date on your state's official portal. Seeded with a fictional sample portfolio: 4 entities across TX/WY/DE, one annual report due in 9 days, one agent renewal overdue, one intercompany loan with a balance, and a 60/40 ownership split.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no state filing, no portal automation, no live status lookups (it records your confirmations, never represents that an entity IS in good standing), not a registered-agent service, no accounting, no legal or tax advice, no document generation for filings.

**Private by design:** your whole structure — arguably the most local data a business has — stays in local JSON on your PC. EINs are stored locally, masked by default, shown only on deliberate reveal, and excluded from exports unless you opt in. (Sample data uses obvious 00-0000000 fakes.) No account, no cloud, no telemetry, works fully offline in 100 languages.

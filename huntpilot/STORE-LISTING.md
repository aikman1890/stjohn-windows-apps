# HuntPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $29.99/month membership — flat, one ranch, every hunt and every trophy included. No percentage of any hunt, ever.

**Tagline:** The hunt and trophy book for exotic-game ranches: every hunter, every species and season, every balance — and every trophy tracked from field dressing to the client's wall.

**What it is:** Ranches sell four- and five-figure hunts — trophy fees from $5,500 to $25,000 on top of the package — and the new cloud tools stop at booking, deposit, and check-in. Everything the client actually judges you on for the next year happens after the harvest, and it has run on paper, memory, and a taxidermist's phone number. HuntPilot is the book of record behind the storefront: a species and season book with your own trophy fees, season and method notes, and herd notes; a hunter file per client across the years — license details as you record them, party members, preferences and wishlist, past hunts and lifetime value; a hunt book with guides, lodging nights and non-hunter guests, a waiver status register (fact and version of signing), and a balance ledger where the deposit lands at booking, trophy fees are added as animals are harvested, and the balance is always arithmetic instead of a notebook; a harvest log whose entries open trophy cases automatically; the trophy chain tracker — field dressed, at processor (date and ticket), meat processed and shipped, at taxidermist (date and work order), mount in progress, mount complete, crated, shipped, delivered — every stage carrying a days-in-stage clock against your own norm, orange past it, red past double, so nothing sits forgotten at a taxidermist for a year; a follow-up list that answers "who do I call today"; a guide and lodge board by day; and a season report — hunts, harvests by species, revenue, balances outstanding, trophies outstanding by stage, and the repeat-marketing list — in one export. Seeded with a fictional sample ranch: three priced species, three hunters (one license inside its renewal window), and three trophies at different chain stages, one already red.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no online booking, no deposits, no payments, and no client portal (the cloud storefronts can keep that fight); no hunting-regulation or license advice — it records what the ranch enters and never interprets any state's rules; no herd-management or wildlife-biology suite.

**Private by design:** local JSON on the ranch's own computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — fully offline in the hill country, where the office is the system of record and a high-value client list is not rented back from a startup's cloud, in 100 languages.

# PlotPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $9.99/month membership — flat, no per-plot tiers.

**Tagline:** Records that outlive us all, kept by you — the small cemetery's plots, burials, and deeds reconciled into one searchable record on the office's own computer.

**What it is:** Cemify, the cheapest verified cloud floor, is $599 a year with plot-count tiers; CemeteryBase is $99 a month; the rest are quote-only. Meanwhile the documented reality at small cemeteries is an old map and several old notebooks. PlotPilot is those notebooks finished: a plot register on your own addressing scheme (section / block / lot / space, exactly as your plat names them) with Available / Sold / Reserved / Occupied — and Unknown as a first-class status, because old records have gaps and the app represents them honestly instead of forcing false precision; burial records with funeral home, marker notes, veteran flags, and the source notebook and page every entry came from; a dated ownership and deed chain per space (sales, transfers, inheritances, perpetual-care contributions recorded per sale) that answers who may authorize a burial in this space; a section map rendered from the register itself — the map is the book, so the two can never drift apart — printable for the grounds; counter search by decedent, owner, or address, plus available spaces near a family plot; a reconciliation view that lists where plat, notebooks, and register disagree for the board to work through deliberately; a sexton's work log; reports by year and section; and a complete plain CSV export — because the cemetery's records must outlive any vendor, including this one. Seeded with a fictional sample cemetery: 60 spaces, 14 burials with notebook provenance, and one deliberate plat-vs-notebook conflict waiting in the reconciliation view.

**What it is not:** no GIS or survey-grade mapping, no GPS grave-finding, no public find-a-grave website, no funeral-home case management, no accounting beyond the record (the funds' books stay with the treasurer), and no legal determination of burial rights — it shows the recorded chain and flags conflicts; disputed rights are for the board and its counsel.

**Private by design:** local JSON storage on the office PC. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, works fully offline in 100 languages. When a subscription elsewhere lapses, the map disappears; yours doesn't.

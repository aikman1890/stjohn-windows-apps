# EstatePilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $39.99/month membership — flat, every property included. No 12-month pre-pay, no onboarding fee, no per-household accounts.

**Tagline:** The estate manager's private book — properties, people, vendors, clocks, and the house manual, on your machine and nowhere else.

**What it is:** The category's platform sells households a collaboration system on a 12-month pre-paid commitment with an onboarding fee — and puts each family's manual, staff details, and vendor list in a vendor's cloud, with accounts for everyone. The independent manager serving one to four families still runs on a binder, a spreadsheet, and a phone full of contacts. EstatePilot is that binder finished: a property file per residence (systems, documents vault of local paths, access recorded as pointers only); a maintenance clock engine for HVAC, generator, pool, chimney, septic, and elevator services with green/orange/red countdowns; a staff book with schedule patterns and the document clocks the manager tracks; a vendor book where the certificate-of-insurance expiry is a first-class clock, so the list itself shows who is currently insured to work in the house; a searchable house manual — room notes, standing instructions, principal preferences, seasonal procedures — exportable for handover and immune to staff turnover; a job ledger that answers "when was the roof last done and what did it cost?" with warranty clocks; and reusable arrival/departure/open/close checklists whose runs are kept as dated proof. Seeded with one fictional sample residence: an overdue generator service, an HVAC clock in its orange window, two vendors (one COI expiring in 16 days), two staff, and a starter manual.

**What it is not — stated plainly, including the safety line:** no staff accounts, messaging, or task assignment; no payroll, timekeeping, or HR; no financial management; no smart-home or security integration — and EstatePilot NEVER stores alarm codes, safe combinations, gate codes, or passwords. It records that a system exists and who services it; access is a pointer ("keys held by the house manager"), never the keys themselves. Not a rental property-management system: no tenants, no rent roll.

**Private by design:** an estate's records — addresses, occupancy patterns, staff, preferences — are among the most sensitive operational data a family has. They live in local JSON on the manager's machine. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, fully offline in 100 languages. The discretion is the product.

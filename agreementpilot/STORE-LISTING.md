# AgreementPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $14.99/month membership

**Tagline:** The equipment-and-agreement book for small trade shops — every unit at every address, every renewal counted down, every tune-up owed listed, every aging system named before it fails.

**What it is:** ServiceTitan does equipment history and agreement automation at a reported $245–$500 per tech per month with five-figure implementation. Jobber, the affordable default, has no equipment tracking tied to service history and no maintenance-agreement automation — its own reviewers call the workaround duct tape. AgreementPilot is exactly the missing book, running locally on the shop's own PC: equipment per service address with age computed and full service history per unit; the maintenance-agreement register with renewal countdowns (green / orange / red, thresholds you set) and visits included vs. delivered; a tune-ups-owed board grouped by season so the slow-season call list builds itself; a replacement watch that flags units crossing your age threshold (15/20 years) with their history summary; a service-visit log whose recommendations carry the customer's approved/declined answer — the declined register, with follow-up tracking, so "we told them in May" survives to August; an honest agreement-profitability view (price vs. visits delivered and discounts honored, labeled operational, not accounting); and a printable renewal packet per customer plus a CSV export of the agreement book. Seeded with a fictional sample shop — one agreement expiring, one 19-year-old furnace on the watch — so the pipeline is visible before you enter anything real.

**What it is not:** no dispatch board, no scheduling, no tech mobile app, no invoicing or payments, no flat-rate price book or parts catalog (all your prices and recommendations are your own free text), no customer marketing automation, and no deferred-revenue accounting. It is the back-office book that sits beside whatever you invoice with.

**Private by design:** local JSON storage on your PC. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, works fully offline in 100 languages. Your customer list never leaves the shop.

# LienPilot - Store Listing Draft

**Tagline:** The lien file that runs itself - rent roll, statutory clock, and proof ledger for one facility.

**Price:** $19.99/month

**Description:**
LienPilot is the delinquency-and-lien core for an independent self-storage facility - the part of the big platforms a 100-300 unit operator actually lives in, without the per-user platform price. The rent roll shows every unit, tenant, rate, paid-through date, and balance, flowing through the real pipeline: current, late, lien pending, notice sent, advertised, sale scheduled, released, sold, vacated.

The delinquency clock is the heart. Enter your state's lien steps once - as YOUR profile, from your statute and your counsel, confirmed by you - and LienPilot computes each delinquent tenant's timeline from the default date and your actual ledger entries: when the notice is due, when publication runs, and the earliest sale date, with every date showing the basis it came from. No silent math, and no shipped law: LienPilot contains no state's rules, only a clearly-labeled sample profile you replace and confirm.

The notice proof ledger logs every step with date, delivery method, and tracking - and the sale-ready check reads it. A unit cannot show as cleared to sell while a required step is unlogged, the sale date is unreached, a hold flag (bankruptcy, military, dispute - all set by you) stands, or your profile is unconfirmed; the worksheet says exactly why each held unit is held. Itemized claim statements build from your own fee schedule exactly as the notices state them, notice and advertisement templates merge from the unit file, and the proceeds log tracks sale price, amount applied, and excess proceeds held versus claimed - the file a wrongful-sale defense is made of.

Everything stays on the facility PC in local JSON files. No account, no cloud, no network calls, no payment processing, no gate integration. A sample facility with a mid-lien tenant is seeded on first run so you can watch the clock and the missing-step block work before entering anything real.

**Features:**
- Rent roll / unit board with the full delinquency status pipeline
- Per-tenant lien clock from YOUR confirmed state profile - every date shows its basis
- Notice proof ledger: step, date, delivery method, tracking, proof note
- Sale-ready gate: missing steps, unreached dates, hold flags, and unconfirmed profiles block, with reasons
- Itemized claim statements from your fee schedule, printable
- First notice, lien notice, and sale advertisement templates merged from the unit file
- Sale-day worksheet (cleared vs held) and proceeds / excess-proceeds log
- Per-unit history: every tenancy, notice, and outcome in one file

**Not legal advice:** LienPilot ships no law and represents no timeline as legally sufficient. Profiles are yours to enter, verify, and confirm.

**Privacy:** All data is stored locally on your PC. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

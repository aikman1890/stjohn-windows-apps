**Price:** $9.99/month subscription (cancel anytime)
**Category:** Business
**Keywords:** invoicing, expenses, clients, small business, offline

## Description

BizPilot is an offline-first toolkit built for one-person businesses. Manage
clients, create professional invoices and export them as PDFs, track expenses
with receipt photos, and get help from on-device AI — all without an internet
connection and without your data ever leaving your PC.

## Features

- Professional invoicing with line items, tax, and one-click PDF export
- Expense tracking with receipt photo capture and spending-by-category charts
- On-device AI that drafts client emails and summarizes meeting notes privately

# PlatePilot - Store Listing Draft

**Tagline:** Know what every plate costs - and the day a supplier's price changes, know which dishes break.

**Price:** $9.99/month

**Description:**
PlatePilot is the costing book for a small restaurant, cafe, food truck, or caterer. Build your ingredient price book once - purchase unit, price, and how many base units it contains - and PlatePilot converts everything to a true cost per ounce, pound, or each.

Cost recipes the way kitchens actually lose money: each line carries an as-purchased quantity and the edible-portion yield you measured, so trim and prep loss are priced into the plate. Every dish shows its plate cost and food-cost percentage against your target, live, the moment any price changes.

Prices change constantly, so PlatePilot keeps the history: record a new price and it is dated, noted, and remembered. The price-change impact view answers the question that matters - a supplier raises chicken 12%; which dishes blow their target at the current menu price? - before you reprint anything. A dashboard keeps the worst offenders on top, catering quotes build per-guest pricing straight from costed dishes (with the target-margin price shown), and cost sheets and quotes export as printable documents.

Every number in the app comes from prices you type in. PlatePilot fetches nothing, logs into nothing, and never invents a price - your book, your suppliers, your kitchen. All data stays on your PC in local JSON files, and a small sample price book and recipe are seeded on first run so you can watch the math work.

**Features:**
- Ingredient price book with purchase-unit conversion to cost per base unit
- Dated price history on every ingredient, with change percentages
- Yield-adjusted recipe costing (AP quantity divided by edible-portion yield)
- Live plate cost and food-cost % vs target on every dish
- Price-change impact view: re-costs every affected dish before you commit
- Catering quotes with per-guest pricing and target-margin hints
- Printable cost sheets and quote documents
- All data local; no account, no cloud, no network calls

**Privacy:** All data is stored locally on your PC. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

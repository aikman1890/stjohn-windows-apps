# wuse — Microsoft Store listing

## Short description

wuse is a friendly AI assistant that runs 100% on your Windows PC. Chat, get
things done, and stay private — no cloud, no accounts, no tracking.

## Description

wuse is a warm, capable, slightly playful assistant that lives entirely on
your device. It chats using on-device AI when your PC supports it, and keeps
working in local mode when it doesn't — opening apps, searching the web, and
handling quick tasks. Your conversations never leave your PC: no cloud, no
accounts, no tracking.

## Features

- **100% on-device:** chat and quick actions run locally; nothing you type is
  uploaded anywhere.
- **A do-things assistant:** open apps, search the web, open folders, and check
  machine info — right from the chat box.
- **Your rules:** rename the assistant, pick light/dark/system theme, and teach
  it facts about you — all stored locally on your PC.

## Price

- **wuse Personal** — $4.99/month subscription (cancel anytime)
- **wuse Business** — $9.99/month subscription (cancel anytime): everything in
  Personal, plus up to 5 Microsoft work accounts (Office 365), the password
  vault, and priority voice.

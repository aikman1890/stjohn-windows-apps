# RemodelPilot - Store Listing Draft

**Tagline:** Know if the job is making money while you can still do something about it.

**Price:** $19.99/month

**Description:**
RemodelPilot is the job-money core for a small remodeler or residential contractor - estimate, budget, change orders, actuals, draws, and warranty - without the $499/month platform or the CRM that omits job costing entirely.

Every job gets a file: client, address, contract, status from bid to warranty. The estimate builder prices lines your way - your cost, your markup - and versions are preserved, so what was bid and when survives every renegotiation. Mark the winning version and it becomes the contract price AND the category budget in one move.

Then the money stays live. Actual costs land against the budget by category as the job runs; an overrun flags the moment it happens, not at month-end. The dashboard shows every active job's live margin, and the number contractors say matters most: change orders awaiting approval - money sitting unsigned.

Change orders are priced, dated, and recorded with who approved, when, and how. Approved COs raise the contract price automatically; declined ones stay on file, so the scope conversation is documented either way. Print a CO with a signature line straight from the record. Draws run on a schedule with invoiced and paid states and an overdue-to-invoice view, so draws stop drifting. The sub ledger tracks agreed price, paid to date, and lien-waiver/certificate receipts per job. Punch items close out the job, and completed jobs roll into a warranty book with end dates and a callback log.

All data stays on your PC in local JSON files. No account, no cloud, no portal, no per-seat meter - and decimal money math throughout, so margins never float-round. A sample kitchen remodel with approved, declined, and awaiting-signature change orders is seeded on first run so you can see the whole system work immediately.

**Features:**
- Job files with the full pipeline: bid, won, in progress, punch, complete, warranty
- Estimate builder with your categories, your markups, and preserved versions
- Budget vs. actual job costing with live margin and category-overrun flags
- Change-order register with approval log (who, when, how) and printable COs
- Draw schedule with invoiced/paid states, overdue-to-invoice view, final statement export
- Sub & supplier ledger with balances and waiver/certificate tracking
- Punch list and warranty book with callback log
- Dashboard: live margins, unsigned-CO dollars, outstanding draws, expiring warranties

**Privacy:** All data is stored locally on your PC. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.

# RangePilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $29.99/month membership — flat, one location, every booking included. No percentage of anything, ever.

**Tagline:** The day book for experience attractions: every booking, every signed waiver, every vehicle and lane, every safety officer, and every photo package owed — in one book, on your own machine.

**What it is:** The booking engines charge about 6% of every ticket and stop at the sale — on a $4,950 package that is roughly $297 per booking, forever — and none of them knows your Sherman is down, your 1 PM party has two unsigned waivers, or the $75 video package from Saturday was never delivered. RangePilot is the operations book behind whichever booking channel you keep: a day board that reads the whole operating day at the morning huddle (bookings by slot with balances, waiver status green only when every participant is signed, assets up or down); a booking book with your own packages and deposit/balance ledgers, with source tracking (direct, phone, Groupon, LivingSocial, auction, gift card) so discount leakage shows up in dollars, plus a gift-card issuance and redemption ledger; a waiver register tied to the booking and the party — it records the fact and version of signing from your existing paper or e-waiver process and gates the slot, no clipboard reconciliation; an asset book where a down vehicle or lane blocks new bookings against it and flags the bookings that need a swap or a call-ahead; a staff and certification roster so a slot is ready only when its certified roles are filled; a media ledger that chases every photo and video package owed (orange at two days, red when overdue); a guest book with a win-back list sorted by days since the last visit, so you market to your own guests instead of buying them back at a third off; and a day-close report — revenue by package and source, media outstanding, asset downtime — for the owner's weekly review. Seeded with a fictional sample day: two vehicles (one down), three bookings, one party short two waivers, and one video package already overdue.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no online booking and no payments (keep your booking channel; this runs your day); no e-signature capture (it registers waiver status only); no firearms or vehicle compliance advice — it tracks your own maintenance and certification records and never interprets ATF, state, or insurance rules; no point-of-sale or merchandise inventory.

**Private by design:** local JSON on the operator's own computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — fully offline at the range, where the office PC is the system of record, in 100 languages.

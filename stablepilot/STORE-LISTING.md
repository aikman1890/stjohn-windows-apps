# StablePilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $29.99/month membership — flat, one barn, every horse included. No per-feature metering.

**Tagline:** The horse book for boarding and training barns: every horse, every care clock the law and the calendar impose, and a bill that's right — on the barn office computer, signal or no signal.

**What it is:** The platforms sell lesson barns portals, online booking, and per-feature add-ons (per-feature pricing from $10/feature/month; typical configurations $25–$249/mo) — while the private boarding/training barn runs on a whiteboard, a feed chart, and a paper health binder, for owners who pay four figures a month per horse and expect a private banker's standard of records. StablePilot is that system finished: a horse file per horse (owner, stall, feed program, turnout group, blanket rules, vet/farrier assignments, document vault for Coggins and health papers); a care clock engine where the Coggins runs on its 12-month statutory cycle with a 30-day warning band and farrier/vaccination/deworming/dental cycles warn at 14 — an expired Coggins flags the horse as not travel-legal before it is loaded, not after; a feed and turnout board designed for paper first, printed for the aisle with supplement sign-offs; an append-only health and incident log plus training/session logs that feed the bill; a board billing ledger (monthly board + itemized extras + sessions, decimal math) that generates the per-owner monthly statement instead of the hand-built spreadsheet; a provider book with next rounds booked against the clocks; and a travel-readiness view that checks a whole barn against a show date from its own records. Seeded with a fictional sample barn: four horses, two owners, one Coggins inside its orange window, one farrier overdue, and sessions already feeding a statement.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no lesson booking, no client portals, no online payments, no owner app; no veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations (it tracks the schedules and records your vet sets); no breeding management, no sales/auction tools, and no cattle or livestock production features — this is a sport and boarding horse book.

**Private by design:** local JSON on the barn's own computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — fully offline in the barn office, where connectivity is worst and the book is needed most, in 100 languages.

# GuardPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $14.99/month membership — flat, all sites and all guards included. The anti-per-site price is the point.

**Tagline:** The office system of record for small guard companies — posts, orders, rosters, incidents, and billing hours in one place, at a published flat price with no contract.

**What it is:** TrackTik doesn't publish pricing (third-party listings suggest ~$78/mo per location, estimate-grade); Silvertrac reports run per-device; OfficerApps from ~$70/site/mo. So small firms run posts on paper and WhatsApp — the roster in a spreadsheet, post orders in a binder, incident reports in a folder, invoice hours reconstructed at month-end. GuardPilot is that binder finished: client sites and posts with contract bill rates; a post-orders book where every revision is versioned — the orders in force on a given night are retrievable verbatim — with printable binder sheets; a guard roster whose licence expiries warn at 30 days, orange then red, before they become a compliance incident; a weekly schedule board that flags unfilled shifts, double-bookings, licence-expired-on-shift, and overtime accumulation on its own; a searchable incident log with follow-up actions, owners, due dates, and overdue flags — the artifact clients and insurers actually ask for; a DAR intake log that turns paper, texts, and call-ins into searchable records; and a billing ledger of scheduled vs. actual hours per site at contract rates, invoice-ready, with guard pay-hour totals for payroll prep. Seeded with a fictional sample firm: 4 sites, 6 guards, a week scheduled with one unfilled shift, one licence expiring in 12 days, and two incidents — one follow-up overdue.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no GPS patrol verification, no checkpoint scanning, no live guard tracking, no guard-facing mobile app. That field proof needs phones and a network and is the platforms' real product; GuardPilot is the office record, and it does not fake the field half. No payroll processing and no invoicing engine (it produces the verified hours; InvoicePilot or your accounting tool issues the invoice), no licence verification with any state and no compliance certification — licence fields are your own records with expiry warnings, and no background checks or applicant tracking.

**Private by design:** local JSON storage on your PC — client lists and incident narratives stay in your office. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, works fully offline in 100 languages.

# CharterPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $39.99/month membership — flat, every quote and client included.

**Tagline:** In charter brokerage the money is lost in the follow-up, not the flying. This is the book that follows up.

**What it is:** The aviation platforms sell to operators — €99 per user per month gets a broker a seat inside someone else's system of record, holding the operator's fleet data, not the broker's book. So independent brokers run their business from an inbox: forty quotes out, no record of which are still alive, margins in a spreadsheet updated when remembered, and client preferences — the entire moat — in one person's head. CharterPilot is the broker's own system of record: a quote log where every sent quote carries a follow-up clock (4 hours, same-day, next-morning presets) in green/orange/red — the dashboard is literally a list of quotes that will die today unless touched, and touching one restarts its clock; a client book with bookers/assistants, preferences (aircraft class, cabin, catering, FBO, ground transport), quote history, and lifetime margin; an operator and tail vendor book carrying your own notes on reliability, catering standard, and cancellation behavior; trip files with the six-item readiness checklist (signed agreement, operator confirmation, passenger list, catering, ground transport, payment) that refuse to call a trip ready with an item unlogged; a margin ledger computed from the records — never typed, so it can't disagree with itself — rolled up per client, per operator, per month, in explicit multi-currency decimals; and a lost-quote register with reason codes (price, timing, no response, aircraft unavailable, another broker) that shows your real loss pattern. One saved mid-size trip pays for years of membership. Seeded with a fictional sample book: two clients, two operators, two tails, three quotes at three clock states (one follow-up already overdue), and one trip departing in about 30 hours with its file incomplete.

**What it is not — stated plainly:** no flight operations, dispatch, or crew-duty/FTL tracking; no marketplace access or live availability; no payments or client portals; no quoting engine — you enter the quotes you actually sent, and CharterPilot owns the record and the clocks. Flight operations and safety remain the operator's responsibility.

**Private by design:** client lists and margins are the most commercially sensitive data a broker owns, and they never leave his machine — local JSON, no account, no cloud, no telemetry, fully offline in 100 languages.

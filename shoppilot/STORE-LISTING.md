# ShopPilot — Gumroad listing (stub)

**Price:** $19.99/month membership — flat, every seat in the shop included. There is no per-user pricing in this product, on purpose.

**Tagline:** Quote it, win it, run it on the board, print the traveler, cost it when it ships — the job-shop record for 2–15-person shops, on your own computer.

**What it is:** JobBOSS² publishes no price (third-party estimates run $85–$200 per user per month plus $5,000–$40,000+ implementation), ProShop estimates start around $500/month, and the cheapest credible cloud MRP is $49 per user per month — per-user pricing is exactly why small shops "give logins to the office only and keep the floor on paper." ShopPilot is the layer those shops actually run on: a quote log with win/loss and loss reasons, win rate by customer and by period, and your cost estimates beside every price; job cards created from won quotes with the full operation route (your own operation library), material and cert references, customer PO, due date and priority; a scheduling board by work center in priority order with the green/orange/red due language — leave it on a wall monitor; a printed paper traveler with sign-off boxes, because paper on the floor is a feature when the system generates it; job costing that finally reconciles actual vs. quoted — material, outside services, and labor hours from the traveler sign-offs at your shop rate, variance shown plainly; an outside-services log (plating, heat treat) with promised vs. actual return dates and overdue flags; customer and repeat-part history that answers "what did we charge for this bracket in March, and did we make money?"; and ship/close-out with invoice references. Seeded with a fictional sample shop (3 customers, 5 quotes, jobs mid-route, one closed job with a margin variance) so the costing screen teaches itself.

**What it is not:** no CAD/CAM or machine integration, no full MRP/ERP (no inventory optimization, no BOM explosion, no accounting, no payroll), no customer portal or cloud sync, and no quality-certification anything — it records sign-offs and cert references; it certifies nothing. Invoicing stays with InvoicePilot or your accounting tool; ShopPilot ends at ship + invoice reference.

**Private by design:** local JSON storage on the shop's PC. No account, no cloud, no telemetry, works fully offline in 100 languages. Your jobs never leave your computer.

# MASTER GAP MAP — All 20 NAICS Sectors

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Prepared for:** Shaun Nicholas St. John — St. John Windows apps (WinUI 3, local-first, Gumroad membership) + St. John Pictures / game factory
**Method:** Systematic sweep of all 20 NAICS sectors against the house model: small operators (1–20 staff) priced out of per-user/per-location cloud tools or still on paper/spreadsheets, whose core record-keeping is office/desktop work that does not inherently need a network. Pricing verified from vendor pages, review/comparison sites, or fetched listings on 2026-10-25; anything not vendor-verified is labeled ESTIMATE. Games judged against the 184-title factory catalog plus Steam/itch.io incumbents.

**Verdict counts:** App gap YES in **12 of 20 sectors** (11, 21, 23, 31-33, 42, 54, 55, 56, 61, 71, 81, 92) — plus a narrow warehousing slice in 48-49. Game gap YES in **9 of 20 sectors** (11, 21, 42, 51, 54, 56, 61, 71, 81). **Neither** in 5 sectors (22, 44-45, 53, 62, 72). The rest of the sectors are covered, in an active hunt, or dropped with reasons (below).

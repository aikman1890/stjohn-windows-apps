# TruckPilot - Store Listing Draft

**Tagline:** IFTA records an owner-operator can actually keep - and an audit file that assembles itself.

**Price:** $14.99/month

**Description:**
TruckPilot is the record book for a one-truck IFTA operation. Log every trip segment by jurisdiction with odometer start and end - the app flags gaps and overlaps the moment you type them, while you still remember what those miles were. Log every fuel purchase with gallons, cost, and receipt reference; missing receipts get flagged before an auditor finds them.

Each quarter, TruckPilot runs the standard IFTA worksheet on your records: total miles, total gallons, average MPG, taxable gallons per jurisdiction, less tax-paid gallons, times the rate - producing the per-jurisdiction figures you transcribe onto your base jurisdiction's return.

The rate table is yours. TruckPilot ships with NO tax rates, on purpose: you copy each quarter's rates from the official IFTA rate chart, enter them, and confirm them. Only confirmed rates are ever used, unconfirmed rows stay visibly unpriced, and last quarter's rate is never silently carried forward. The printed worksheet includes the exact rate table used, with confirmation dates and sources.

When the letter comes, the audit export assembles the complete year - profile, every trip, every fuel purchase, every rate entry, all four quarterly worksheets - into one printable document. A retention view shows how far back your file reaches against the general 4-year IFTA record window. Nothing is ever deleted automatically, and everything lives on your PC in local JSON files. No account, no cloud, no network calls.

**Honest limits:** TruckPilot does not file your return and is not tax advice. Fuel-tax law varies by jurisdiction and changes over time - verify against your base jurisdiction's current rules and consult a qualified tax professional.

**Features:**
- Trip log by jurisdiction with odometer continuity warnings as you type
- Fuel log with receipt references and missing-receipt flags
- Quarterly IFTA worksheet: MPG, taxable gallons, net tax per jurisdiction
- Operator-confirmed rate table - no shipped rates, no silent carry-forward
- Printable worksheet export showing the rate table used
- Full-year audit packet export (trips, fuel, rates, all four worksheets)
- 4-year retention status at a glance; nothing auto-deleted
- All data local on your PC; the app makes no network calls

**Privacy:** All data is stored locally on your PC. The app makes no network calls and collects nothing.
